# Financial Year Management

## Key Message

Financial year status controls when budgets can be created, reviewed, linked to POs, and closed.

## Slide Content

- Purpose: establish the official annual budget control period
- Process: OPEN -> PRE-CLOSING -> CLOSED
- OPEN: departments can create and submit budgets
- PRE-CLOSING: closure preparation begins; unresolved approvals, transfers, and PO links must be addressed
- CLOSED: the year is locked for governance and historical reporting

Three-stage timeline with allowed and restricted actions under each state.

## Speaker Notes

- Closure is blocked until required items are resolved.
- This reduces open-ended financial activity.

# Price Intelligence Metrics

## Key Message

Each metric explains price reasonableness, evidence quality, and financial impact in simple business terms.

## Slide Content

- Historical Benchmark: typical unit price based on median historical unit cost
- Variance %: ((Budget Unit Price - Benchmark) / Benchmark) x 100
- Potential Overspend: max(0, Budget Unit Price - Benchmark) x Quantity
- Evidence Strength: confidence based on purchase count, supplier count, and recency
- Example: (SAR 420 - SAR 390) x 50 = SAR 1,500 potential overspend

## Suggested Visual

Metric card grid with formula callouts and one worked example.

## Speaker Notes

- Median is used because it reduces outlier distortion.
- Variance shows unit-price difference.
- Potential overspend converts unit variance into total budget exposure.
